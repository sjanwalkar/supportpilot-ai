# Newsletter and Communication Preferences

Category: Newsletter

## Subscribing to the newsletter

You can subscribe to the Northwind newsletter during checkout (there's a
checkbox, unchecked by default) or anytime from Account Settings >
Communication Preferences. The newsletter covers new product launches and
occasional discount codes, sent roughly twice a month.

## Unsubscribing

Every newsletter email has an "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom that takes
effect immediately. You can also manage this from Account Settings >
Communication Preferences without needing to open an email.

## Order and shipping notifications are separate

Unsubscribing from the newsletter does not affect order confirmation,
shipping, or delivery emails — those are considered account notifications,
not marketing, and stay on since they're needed to track your purchases.
