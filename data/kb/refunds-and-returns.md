# Refunds and Returns

Category: Refunds

## Refund policy

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they're unused and in original packaging. Perishable goods,
gift cards, and personalized items are final sale and not eligible for
return.

## Starting a return

Start a return under Account > Orders > select the order > Request
Return. You'll get a prepaid return shipping label by email. Drop the
package at any carrier location within 14 days of requesting the return.

## Refund timelines

Once we receive and inspect a returned item (usually 2-3 business days
after it arrives at our warehouse), the refund is issued to your original
payment method. Refunds typically appear within 5-10 business days after
that, depending on your bank or card issuer's processing time.

## Tracking a refund

Check refund status under Account > Orders > select the order — it will
show as "Refund Requested," "Refund Processing," or "Refund Completed."
If it's been more than 10 business days since "Refund Completed" and you
still don't see the funds, contact support with your order number.
