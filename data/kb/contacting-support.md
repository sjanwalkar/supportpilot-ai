# Contacting Support and Sharing Feedback

Category: Contact

## Contacting customer service

Reach Northwind support by email or live chat, both linked from the Help
Center. Live chat is staffed 8am-8pm ET, Monday-Saturday. Email replies
typically arrive within 1 business day.

## Reaching a human agent

Our chat assistant can answer most questions about orders, shipping,
payments, and refunds directly. For anything involving account security,
a dispute, or a request the assistant can't resolve, ask to speak with a
human agent and you'll be transferred with your conversation history
included, so you won't have to repeat yourself.

## Filing a complaint

Complaints about a product, an order experience, or service can be filed
through the Help Center's "Report an Issue" form, or by asking support
directly. Complaints are reviewed within 2 business days and you'll
receive a follow-up email with the outcome.

## Leaving product feedback or a review

Reviews can be left on any delivered product from Account > Orders >
select the order > Leave a Review. General product ideas or suggestions
can be submitted through the Help Center's "Share Feedback" form.
