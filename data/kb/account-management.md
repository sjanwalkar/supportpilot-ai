# Managing Your Northwind Account

Category: Account

## Resetting your password

Go to the sign-in page and select "Forgot password." Enter the email
address on your account and we'll send a reset link within a few minutes.
The link expires after 30 minutes — if it expires, just request a new one.
If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder before contacting
support.

## Creating an account

You can create a Northwind account with an email address and password, or
sign in with Google or Apple. No credit card is required to create an
account — you're only asked for payment details when you place your first
order or start a subscription.

## Editing account details

Update your name, email, phone number, or password anytime from
Account Settings. Changing your email address requires confirming the new
address before it takes effect.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is permanent and removes your order history, saved
addresses, and payment methods. Active subscriptions must be canceled
separately first. Request deletion from Account Settings > Privacy, or by
contacting support. Deletion is processed within 5 business days.

## Switching between personal and business accounts

If you have both a personal and a business Northwind account under the
same email, use the account switcher in the top-right menu. Orders and
invoices are kept separate between the two.
