# Payments and Invoices

Category: Payment

## Accepted payment methods

Northwind accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple
Pay. Bank transfers and cash on delivery are not supported. You can save
multiple payment methods and choose a default in Account Settings >
Payment Methods.

## Payment issues

If a payment fails at checkout, it's usually one of: an expired card, a
billing address mismatch, or your bank declining the charge for
suspected fraud (this happens often with first-time purchases from a new
merchant — contacting your bank to approve the charge usually resolves it
fastest). Northwind never charges a card until an order actually ships.

## Getting an invoice

Invoices are available for every order under Account > Orders > select
the order > Download Invoice, available as a PDF. Business accounts can
request invoices addressed to a company name and tax ID — set this under
Account Settings > Billing before placing the order.

## Subscription billing

Subscription charges occur on the same day of the month as your original
signup date. If that date doesn't exist in a given month (like the 31st),
billing occurs on the last day of that month instead.
