# Shipping and Delivery

Category: Delivery

## Delivery options

Northwind offers three delivery speeds at checkout: Standard (5-7 business
days, free over $35), Express (2-3 business days, flat $7.99), and
Next-Day (order by 2pm local time, $14.99, available in most major
metro areas only).

## Delivery timelines

Delivery estimates shown at checkout are calculated from your shipping
address and start counting from when the order ships, not when it's
placed. Processing typically adds 1 business day before an order ships.

## Changing a shipping address

You can update the shipping address only before an order ships (see the
Orders article for the exact cutoff). Once shipped, we're unable to
redirect a package — contact the carrier directly using the tracking
number if you need to intercept a shipment.

## Missing or damaged deliveries

If a package shows as delivered but you can't find it, wait 24 hours (it's
sometimes marked delivered before it physically arrives) and check with
neighbors or building management. If it's genuinely lost or arrived
damaged, contact support with your order number — we'll send a replacement
or refund, no return of the damaged item required for items under $75.
