# Orders: Placing, Tracking, Changing, and Canceling

Category: Orders

## Placing an order

Add items to your cart and check out with a saved or new payment method.
You'll get an order confirmation email immediately, and a second email
once the order ships.

## Tracking your order

Find tracking information under Account > Orders > select the order.
Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after an order ships.
If tracking hasn't updated in more than 3 business days, contact support.

## Changing an order

Orders can be changed (item, quantity, or shipping address) only while
they're still in "Processing" status, usually within 1 hour of placing
the order. Once an order moves to "Preparing for Shipment," it can no
longer be changed — you'll need to cancel and reorder if possible.

## Canceling an order and cancellation fees

Orders canceled while still "Processing" are free to cancel, with a full
refund issued to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.
Orders canceled after they've entered "Preparing for Shipment" incur a
flat $5 cancellation fee to cover picking and packing costs already done.
Orders that have already shipped cannot be canceled — you'll need to
request a return instead once delivered.
